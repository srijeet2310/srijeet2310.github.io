My research interests lie at the intersection of computer architecture, many-core heterogeneous systems, memory-centric computing architectures, and dedicated accelerators, where we can offload computation for higher performance.

I am currently working at the DevTools PerfWorks Team at [NVIDIA Corporation](https://www.nvidia.com/en-in/), where I collaborate with hardware teams to introduce performance-monitoring signals and counters across GPGPU units to collect performance and memory data during kernel execution. These metrics serve as the basis for profiling tools like [Nsight Compute](https://developer.nvidia.com/nsight-compute) and [Nsight Systems](https://developer.nvidia.com/nsight-systems). 

I have worked at the [Adaptive Heterogeneous Systems Laboratory](https://www.hevs.ch/en/collaborateurs/guerrieri-207310), [HES-SO Valais-Wallis](https://www.hevs.ch/en), Switzerland, in collaboration with Professor [Andrea Guerrieri](https://people.hes-so.ch/en/profile/7560748869-andrea-guerrieri), where I developed frameworks to reduce DSE time to converge on the best-quality energy-efficient FPGA-based accelerator designs from high-level code. I pursued my undergraduate thesis at the [Processor Architecture Laboratory](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lap/) at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/), Switzerland, under the guidance of Professors [Paolo Ienne](https://people.epfl.ch/paolo.ienne) and [Andrea Guerrieri](https://people.hes-so.ch/en/profile/7560748869-andrea-guerrieri), where I co-developed and co-authored [DynaRapid](https://sites.google.com/view/dynarapid/) to reduce compilation times of placed and routed kernel designs from C/C++ code for commercial FPGAs by 33x. Our work on DynaRapid received the [Michal Servit Best Paper Award](https://tcfpga.org/books/community-awards/page/fpl-best-paper-awards) at FPL'24.



Previously, I have worked briefly at the IOSXE Polaris Team at [CISCO Systems](https://www.cisco.com/), developing the Internetworking Operating System (IOS) used in all networking devices manufactured by CISCO and have interned at the [Edinburgh Architecture and Systems Laboratory](https://easelab.inf.ed.ac.uk/) under the guidance of Professor [Boris Grot](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/bgrot/) at the [University of Edinburgh](https://www.ed.ac.uk/), where I worked on caches and hardware prefetchers. I have completed my undergraduate in Electrical and Electronics Engineering at the [Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/pilani). During my undergraduate, I have led [Team Anant](https://www.team-anant.com/) and contributed to their mission to build nanosatellites to detect global pollution hotspots.
